Diagnostics 

      

      
If captions ever seem stuck, tap the screen once: that restarts the microphone. Tap anywhere on the captions to show or hide this panel. Gray words are still being recognized and may change. Captions keep running while this panel is open, and resume on their own after the phone wakes. If iOS won’t allow that, a “Tap to resume” button appears.
